Reading the timeline

Library and Archives Canada selects a particular page to preserve. Beneath it, each year links to one Internet Archive capture of the original URL, recorded on or before the featured capture. The two archives have different collections. Gaps can reflect capture coverage.

For government-controlled addresses we also check the original path over HTTPS. A response or redirect records how that URL responded at the check. “Archive only” marks former addresses outside the government namespaces we check. Some historical commission reports share a host and have separate paths.
